The Norwegian Civil Affairs Authority defines the guardianship authorisations that determine what a guardian may do on behalf of a person under guardianship. Altinn Authorisation obtains guardianship information from the National Population Register. The table under each area lists the defined access packages and links to their current contents in Tjenesteoversikten.
