Altinn Studio is never a finished product, and it will never cover 100% of your needs out of the box. But the tool takes you a long way towards building good digital services, and we keep adding new functionality in response to feedback. Where the tool falls short, you can build the missing pieces yourself.

Instead of building your own or buying something – why not contribute to the project where something is missing? That way you give something back. The Altinn Studio teams handle quality assurance and ongoing maintenance of the code.

## Community around service development
It is important to us to build a community around the work of developing services. We now have many contributors from various public agencies who help us advance the product. Contributions range from typos that take seconds to fix, to large components that have taken months to develop.

## Slack and GitHub
An important part of the community is the developer contact we have via [Digdir-samarbeid on Slack](https://digdir-samarbeid.slack.com/). Each service owner has their own channel for asking questions. We also see service owners helping each other and sharing experiences across organisations.

Everything is openly available on [GitHub](https://github.com/Altinn/altinn-studio): backlog, user stories, plans and roadmap. There you can both follow along and comment.
