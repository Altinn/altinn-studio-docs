In the browser you log in with ID-porten and act on behalf of an organisation. People can send and receive files without connecting their own system to the API.

When a system sends and receives files, follow the [Broker developer guide](/en/broker/getting-started/developer-guides/).

In the TT02 test environment, open

https://ab.tt02.altinn.no

## Choose a guide

- If you are a service owner, follow [Set up a broker service for the browser](/en/broker/getting-started/web-app/service-owner/).
- If you send or download files, follow [Send and receive files in the browser](/en/broker/getting-started/web-app/use/).
